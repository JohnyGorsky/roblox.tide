# FINDING 0019: A large torque applied in a BODY-relative frame leaks into every other axis the body rotates about - it spun the boat with the wheel amidships

**Project:** `roblox.tide`
**Status:** open
**Severity:** high
**Created:** 2026-08-20 23:56:27

**Symptom:** Root cause of a whole afternoon of apparently unrelated vessel bugs, and the single most valuable lesson from job 021. The trim torque - the bow-up moment under power, 4,090,732 N-stud and by far the largest force on the hull - was applied with RelativeTo = Attachment0 about the hull's LOCAL X axis. That is the intuitive choice: bow-up should stay bow-up as she turns. But the moment the hull ROLLS even a few degrees, local X acquires a world-Y component, and a fraction of a four-million torque is an enormous unintended YAW moment. Measured at 5.6 degrees of roll that is roughly 400,000 of yaw torque, which is nearly three times the rudder's entire authority. And it closes a positive feedback loop: yaw raises the centrifugal heel term, heel rolls her further, more roll leaks more of the trim torque into yaw. Symptom, measured with the wheel DEAD AMIDSHIPS in FLAT WATER at full throttle: she yawed 178 degrees in 8 seconds. The same failure was present in the heel torque, which I fixed first without realising trim had it too - so the fix looked like it had not worked, and the remaining spin got misattributed to wave physics and to gyroscopic coupling. It also produced a string of false leads: turn rate reading 34 to 59 percent of design, yaw exceeding its cap, DeadCalm turning the wrong way, and measurements that contradicted each other run to run, because the leak depended on how rolled she happened to be at the moment of sampling. THE RULE: if a torque should only ever act about ONE world axis, aim it in WORLD space and recompute the axis every frame - for pitch, the hull's starboard vector flattened to horizontal; for roll, the hull's forward vector flattened to horizontal. Body-relative frames are only safe for small torques, or for bodies that cannot rotate about the other axes. Every Torque and VectorForce on the vessel is now RelativeTo World with an explicitly aimed direction, and that audit is worth repeating on any future constrained assembly. Corollary for diagnosis: when several measurements of the same thing disagree between runs, suspect a cross-axis leak whose magnitude depends on attitude, rather than assuming the measurements are noisy.
