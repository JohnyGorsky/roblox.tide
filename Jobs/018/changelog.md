🛠️ Update Notes

🌤️ The sea has moods of its own now — a swell can build, a shower can pass, and none of it means the storm.
🌧️ Rain can fall on a windless afternoon. Rain is weather. It is not a warning.
⛈️ The storm still owns the dark: when the light goes and the sky closes, that one *is* the front.
☁️ Look astern. A wall of cloud gathers back there, and it only ever grows.
⚡ Lightning cracks inside the storm — count the seconds to the thunder and you know how close it is.
🌊 Weather now arrives instead of switching. Every change eases in over nine seconds.
💨 You can hear the wind rise before you can see why.
