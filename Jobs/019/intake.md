# Job #019: Living-world audio: ambience mixer, ocean beds, storm bed

**Project**: `roblox.tide`
**Created**: 2026-08-20 16:40:44
**Status**: ✅ Completed

## Requirements / goal

Group 12's P0 half - everything that needs no vessel - raised to P0 on the user's direction that the game should feel live. Decision 0021 settles the direction: NO MUSIC in the POC (the ocean carries the mood, and if there has never been music then silence is the default rather than an effect), Pro Sound Effects on the Creator Store as the primary source (free, verified, and crucially LONG - 30 to 135 second clips where most Roblox audio is 2 to 5), and long clips preferred over clever code wherever a longer recording exists. SCOPE. (1) An AMBIENCE MIXER, which is the piece that actually makes the world feel alive rather than merely layered: it must crossfade beds by sea state and time of day off the same SeaState and blend attributes the composer uses, so sound and look can never disagree about the weather, and it MUST reuse StormAudio's proven anti-repetition machinery - several voices per bed, equal-power crossfade verified to sum to exactly 1.0 at every phase, random re-seek while a voice is silent, per-voice detune plus slow shared drift. Do not write a second worse version of that. (2) OCEAN BEDS for the five sea states, crossfaded by the same blend. (3) A STORM BED - a new continuous channel, not a replacement for the per-strike thunder: the Pro Sound Effects Thunder clips are 80 to 135 seconds of rolling distant rumble with light rain baked in, which disqualifies them from the one-shot slot where baked-in content fires at the wrong moment, but makes them ideal as an ambient layer levelled off storm intensity. (4) DAY/NIGHT ambience keyed off DayNight.currentPhase - sea birds by day, the different quiet of night. (5) DEAD CALM's bed, which is a design problem rather than a sourcing one: Dead Calm is meant to be the most unsettling state, so its audio must be wrong-sounding rather than absent, because literal silence reads as a bug. (6) SWAP THE RAIN CLIP from our 1.54s upload to the verified 36.01s Pro Sound Effects loop 9112855484, dropping the rain voice count from 4 at plus-minus 13 percent detune to 2 at plus-minus 2 percent - that removes a workaround rather than tuning it, since the wide detune was always the price of a short clip and never a preference. CONSTRAINTS. Weather stays non-positional because it is everywhere at once and a positioned weather emitter gets quieter when the player turns their head. Empty slots hold empty string and never a placeholder id, and must announce themselves via missing(). Rate times lifetime times voices is a performance budget to be read back and checked, not a density dial - the cloud wall taught that twice. Search the library by its own category vocabulary (Thunder, Water - Surf, Boats - Bow Wash); plain-language searches returned subway trains. Licence is Roblox-only use and must be recorded that way in the shared registry. VERIFIED CANDIDATES, all loaded and length-checked: rain 9112855484 at 36.01s, ocean bed 9120621776 at 33.13s, shore wash 9117143192 at 31.51s, storm bed 9120018692 at 109.74s and 9120018411 at 80.94s. Hull wash 9112871242 at 36.01s is confirmed but belongs to the boat half. NOT IN SCOPE: vessel machinery (needs group 02), creature sounds (needs group 05), music of any kind (decision 0021), and the lobby's own bed (deferred to group 08).
