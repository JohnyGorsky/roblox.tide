# Job #021: Vessel foundation: chassis, socket kit, buoyancy, helm and fuel

**Project**: `roblox.tide`
**Created**: 2026-08-20 21:58:19
**Status**: Requirements Gathering (intake)

## Requirements / goal

Group 02's first job, and the first thing that makes this a game rather than a scene. Architecture per decision 0009: build a VESSEL - a chassis plus named sockets, with a module library - and treat the starter launch as the kit's FIRST CUSTOMER rather than the thing being built. The manifest is explicit that building a boat instead is the trap, because seven vessel classes then cost seven boats instead of one hull each. Physics and authority per decision 0022, settled with the user: hull DENSE (CustomPhysicalProperties density above 1) so terrain water does not lift it at all and every stud of lift comes from our own force sampling WaveField.HeightAt - engine buoyancy pulls toward a flat Y=0 plane and would fight the wave field, and the symptom is jitter that reads as a tuning problem rather than a conflict (finding 0008). Hull stays SERVER-OWNED with a plain Seat plus our own throttle/steer RemoteEvent, NOT a VehicleSeat: the roblox-physics skill records that a server loop computing force from a client-owned body pumps energy instead of removing it, which made the Jungle boat bounce higher and higher while driven, and VehicleSeat is what causes it by handing ownership to the driver on sit. Server ownership also matters because StormFront reads the vessel's Z on the server to buy distance from northward progress, so a client-owned hull would feed the game's central mechanic a lagged position. Cost accepted: we build the mobile touch controls ourselves. Four-point buoyancy (bow, stern, port, starboard) so pitch and roll fall out of the wave field's own shape and heading matters, plus a WEAK AlignOrientation righting moment with PrimaryAxisOnly - MaxTorque is the most important feel number on the vessel and wants tuning against Choppy AND TheWall, not LightSwell. Damping must be applied SEPARATELY from the up-only clamp or it vanishes exactly when the boat rises and it bounces forever. Starter launch is 40 x 14 studs with 3 studs of freeboard, chosen against the sea states: dry at Light Swell, spray aboard at Choppy, green water at Storm, swamped in The Wall. SCOPE, as chosen by the user - the larger of the two options: (1) PhysicsChassis and the socket system. (2) Four-point buoyancy on the wave field with correct damping. (3) Thrust and steering, force-based, never teleporting. (4) Custom seat and input remote. (5) ReplicationFocus set to the vessel, not the character - streaming is on so crew far from spawn would watch the deck stream out. (6) Fuel tank, capacity and burn rate, server-authoritative. (7) The helm gauges: speed, fuel, and the COMPASS - which per decision 0014 is the one system that can never fail and must be SELF-LIT and legible in total darkness, because inside The Wall it is one of only two navigation instruments left. Graybox is explicitly not acceptable for the compass: if it cannot be read in the dark it fails its most important job, and The Wall composes Brightness at 0.30. NOT IN SCOPE: the other ~110 manifest items, weapons, radar, generator and power allocation, crew stations, upgrades, and the storm's hull damage - which is deferred group 07 work that needs a hull to damage and should follow this job. VERIFY: multiplayer in Studio, and read numbers back rather than trusting the eye. The break-even helm speed is 8.75 studs per second - at ADVANCE_RATE 14 and GAIN_PER_STUD 1.6 - so the launch's cruise speed relative to that decides whether the game is a chase or a stroll, and it should be chosen deliberately rather than discovered.
