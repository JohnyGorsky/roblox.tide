# Monetization Direction

## Principle

Monetization may make the expedition look cooler and provide more expression/options. It should not sell survival outcomes that free players cannot reasonably achieve.

## Strong monetization areas

### Vessel cosmetics
- hull paint
- flags
- lantern styles
- radar skins
- cabin trim
- rust/weathering themes
- deck decoration
- vessel-specific cosmetic packs

### Character cosmetics
- captain outfits
- raincoats
- diving gear
- expedition uniforms
- emotes

### NPC crew cosmetics
- uniforms
- hats
- visual themes
- voice/dialogue packs if appropriate

### Weapon cosmetics
- harpoon skins
- MG finishes
- flare-gun cosmetics

### Seasonal cosmetic progression
Potential later system:
- free track
- premium cosmetic track
- season-specific vessel appearance
- flags, outfits, emotes, lights

## Do not sell core permanent progression directly

Avoid making the best ship parts/components primarily purchasable with Robux.

Do not sell:
- invincible hull
- best radar from the start
- infinite fuel
- infinite ammo
- huge paid damage modifiers
- pay-only vessel classes that trivialize survival
- paid NPCs that repair dramatically faster than earnable equivalents

## Replay reasons

- collect permanent ship parts
- finish larger vessel construction projects
- improve starting vessel
- different ocean seed/POI combination
- different vessel class
- different run build
- different role mastery
- rare discoveries
- codex completion
- higher difficulty
- seasonal conditions
- prestige cosmetics
- NPC crew collection/development
- social crew play
