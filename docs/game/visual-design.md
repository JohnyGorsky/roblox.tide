# Visual Design

## Direction

**Bermuda military expedition + supernatural ocean survival**

The visual progression should start believable and become increasingly strange.

```text
Faded reality
→ storms and wrecks
→ abandoned military sea
→ cursed water
→ abyss
```

## Palette

### Ocean/world

Listed **darkest first** — the ocean ramp must fall as the sea worsens, and a colour's *name* is not a
reliable guide to where it sits (measured luminance in brackets). This tripped us once: Storm Teal is the
*lightest* of the three despite its name, and using it for a rough sea made the water visibly brighten as
conditions turned nasty. It belongs to **shallow, sheltered water** — the harbour uses it correctly.

- Abyss Navy `#0B1E2D` (0.106) — storm seas, the deep
- Deep Ocean Blue `#12354A` (0.185) — open water, normal conditions
- Storm Teal `#1E5A6E` (0.309) — **shallow/sheltered**, not rough
- Cold Sea Mist `#6E8E99` (0.462) — haze and distance, not water body

### Sky/storm
- Thunder Cloud `#2A313B`
- Wet Steel `#4B5964`
- Fog Grey `#8C9AA3`
- Lightning Pale `#DCEAF2`

### Boat/props
- Weathered Wood `#6B4E3D`
- Rust Brown `#8A5A3A`
- Dark Hull Metal `#2C2F33`
- Old Canvas `#B8B1A1`

### Gameplay accents
- Radar Green `#52FF9A`
- Warning Amber `#F2B544`
- Danger Red `#D94B4B`
- Loot Gold `#D9B65C`

## Usage rule

Approximate visual balance:

- 70% dark blues/greys
- 20% muted support colors
- 10% accent colors

Avoid a bright cheerful tropical-blue Roblox look.

## Day

- Muted teal water
- Faded sunlight
- Old wood, olive/military, rust
- Good visibility
- Warm accents from sun/lanterns

## Night

- Abyss navy and deep ocean blue dominate
- Radar green, amber lamps and red emergency lights become very readable
- More silhouettes than detail
- Fog and reflected lightning create depth

## Storm

- Black/charcoal cloud wall
- Horizontal rain
- White-blue lightning
- Cold mist
- Small warm boat lights for contrast
