## Positioning note

Avoid branding that sounds like a generic "99 Nights on a Raft" clone.

The distinct fantasy should be:
- crew-operated vessel
- persistent fleet
- radar/navigation
- ship systems
- large chasing storm
- progression from small launch to larger vessels/ships
