# Game Vision

## Working title

**The Last Tide**

Other strong candidates are tracked in `naming.md`.

## High concept

A 1–6 player Roblox co-op ocean survival roguelite.

Players begin each expedition with a vessel selected from their persistent fleet. New players begin with a battered motor launch. Over many expeditions they collect permanent upgrade parts and rare components that improve starting vessels and unlock larger vessel classes.

During a run, the crew explores islands, wrecks and sea POIs during the day, gathers fuel/scrap/ammo and permanent ship parts, upgrades the active vessel, and then survives increasingly dangerous nights while a massive supernatural storm advances from behind.

The physical world may be bounded, but horizontal wrapping, curated POI spawning and logical world progression make the ocean feel much larger.

## Long-term vessel fantasy

```text
Old Launch
→ Reinforced Launch
→ Patrol Boat
→ Trawler / Research Vessel
→ Cutter
→ Large Expedition Ship
```

Larger vessels are not simply better:
- consume more fuel
- turn slower
- have more systems
- require more crew attention
- support more stations, weapons, storage and interiors

## NPC crew

NPC crew can fill empty human roles when the expedition has fewer than six players.

They physically perform tasks:
- repair
- radar/navigation
- gunnery
- medicine
- deck work

They should be useful and predictable but not outperform good coordinated human players.

## Fantasy

> Start with a barely functioning vessel. Build a fleet over many expeditions. Turn each run's ship into your crew's home, weapon, shelter and lifeline. Keep moving because the storm is coming.

## Tone

- Cold ocean survival
- Weathered expedition/military equipment
- Tropical decay transitioning into supernatural horror
- Atmospheric rather than gore-focused
- Mystery escalates gradually
- The ocean itself feels dangerous

## Intended player stories

- "We ran out of fuel just before the storm caught us."
- "The radar showed an unknown contact and we risked the detour."
- "We finally found the last component for our Patrol Boat."
- "The engineer NPC kept the pumps alive while we fought on deck."
- "We found the Ghost Lighthouse."
- "The Kraken damaged our generator and we had to turn radar off to keep the pumps running."
- "Our ship looked completely different by the end of the run."

## Target structure

- 1–6 players
- Third-person default
- Contextual first-person for sights/binoculars/guns if useful
- Curated but recombined world content
- Strong co-op roles without forcing hard classes
- NPC role filling for smaller parties
- POC first; longer progression only after the core cycle is proven
