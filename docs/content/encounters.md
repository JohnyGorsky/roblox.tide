# Encounter Design

## Principle

A POI should produce a decision, not only a loot container.

Example:

```text
Radar:
- tanker 1.8 km NE
- small island 0.9 km E
- unknown signal 2.3 km SE
- storm approaching from west
```

Crew must choose what to risk.

## Encounter ingredients

Combine:
- location
- time of day
- weather
- enemy type
- loot tier
- boat condition
- storm distance
- rare modifier

## Example variants

### Fishing village
- calm trader
- pirate-occupied
- abandoned
- drowned at night
- fuel cache
- storm-damaged

### Wreck
- empty salvage
- trapped survivor
- eel threat
- pirate ambush
- rare blueprint
