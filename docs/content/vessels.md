# Vessel Catalog

| Vessel | Role | Suggested crew | Strength | Weakness | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Old Launch | starter / fast scout | 1–4 | speed, low fuel | fragile, low cargo | PLANNED |
| Reinforced Launch | improved starter | 1–4 | balanced early vessel | limited slots | IDEA |
| Patrol Boat | combat/balanced | 2–6 | hardpoints, durability | cargo | IDEA |
| Trawler | salvage/storage | 2–6 | cargo, efficiency | speed, weapons | IDEA |
| Research Vessel | exploration | 2–6 | radar, discovery | armor | IDEA |
| Cutter | heavy survival | 4–6 | hull, power, weapons | fuel, turning | IDEA |
| Expedition Ship | endgame crew vessel | 4–6+NPC | interiors, systems, capacity | complexity, cost | IDEA |

## Rule

Do not implement vessel progression as a simple linear "bigger = always better" ladder.
